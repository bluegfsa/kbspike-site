Privacy Policy — 词桥输入法 (Vocabridge)

Effective: 2026-11-24 | Version: matches submitted build (1.99 / build 103)

In one sentence: everything you type stays on your phone, used only to show candidates and translations locally. We collect no personal data, upload nothing, there is no account, and the keyboard does not need “Full Access”.

Scope

This policy covers the iOS app “词桥输入法 (Vocabridge)”. Provided by an individual developer. Contact: 13872235793lzy@gmail.com (overseas) / 13872235793@163.com (China). Apple’s own services (App Store, iCloud) follow Apple’s privacy policy.

We collect no personal data

The app has no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync. We do not collect, upload or store: your name/phone/email/credentials; anything you type; contacts/photos/microphone/camera/location/clipboard/biometrics; any advertising identifier or cross-app tracking identifier; any usage or crash analytics. The app includes no third-party SDKs (no ads, analytics, push provider, social login or payments).

Where what you type lives

Characters you type are used on-device in real time by the keyboard extension to produce candidates and translations, then discarded — not stored, not uploaded. The Chinese–English dictionaries ship inside the app, offline, not in any cloud. The keyboard does not request “Full Access”, so it has no network capability — what you type cannot leave your device over a network.

No Full Access required

The keyboard extension has RequestsOpenAccess = NO. You do not need to enable “Allow Full Access” to use it.

Deleting your data

We keep none of your data on our side. Deleting the app removes local settings and caches permanently. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include app data in your backup; that backup is controlled and encrypted by your Apple account and we cannot access it.

What we never do

No selling data, no ads or ad SDKs, no cross-app tracking (so no ATT prompt), no access to contacts/photos/location, no silent upload, no training models on your content.

Children

A utility app; App Store age rating 4+ (a content rating, not a claim of being designed for children). A guardian can delete the app to erase its data.

Security

All data stays on-device under iOS sandboxing and file protection; with no account there are no credentials to leak; with no network there is no transmission path.

Changes

Updates change the version and effective date at the top; older versions stay available. Any expansion of collection (e.g. accounts, cloud sync, online translation) will be announced in-app and consented to separately before it takes effect.
